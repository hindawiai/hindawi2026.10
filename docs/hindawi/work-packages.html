Work packages

Pieces of Hindawi anyone can take: a student, a linguist, or an AI working for one. Claiming a package opens an issue on hindawiai/hindawi2026.10, so two people do not take the same one. A language package is the words that language's standard still needs; when it is filled, hindawi standard import checks every word and puts it into the language. Every word stays proposed until the language's community reviews it on the review page.
